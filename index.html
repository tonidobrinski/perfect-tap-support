About the Game

What is Perfect Tap?

A fast-paced arcade experience built to push your reflexes to the absolute limit.

🎮

The Premise

Perfect Tap: Reflex Challenge is an addictive arcade mobile game where every millisecond counts. Tap at precisely the right moment to score, chain perfect hits, and climb your personal high score leaderboard. Simple to learn — impossibly hard to master. Whether you have 30 seconds or 30 minutes, the challenge never gets old.

⚡

Lightning-Fast Gameplay

The game tests your reaction speed and timing with increasingly demanding reflex challenges. Each round is intense, short, and endlessly replayable.

🏆

Beat Your High Score

Compete against yourself — every session is a chance to surpass your personal best. Climb the ranks through pure skill and muscle memory.

🪙

Earn & Spend Coins

Earn coins through gameplay achievements and milestones. Spend them on in-game rewards and power-ups to enhance your experience.

🎁

Rewarded Ads & Coin Packs

Watch optional rewarded ads to earn bonus coins for free, or grab a coin pack through in-app purchase for instant currency boosts without any wait.

Help Center

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions about Perfect Tap.

Perfect Tap is simple but challenging. When the game starts, a target or indicator appears on screen — tap at the exact right moment to score a "Perfect Tap." The closer your timing is to the ideal moment, the higher your score. As you progress, the timing windows get narrower and the pace increases. Your goal is to chain as many perfect taps as possible and beat your personal best score.
Coins can be earned in several ways: completing game rounds and hitting score milestones, watching optional rewarded video ads (found in the main menu or after a round ends), and purchasing coin packs through in-app purchases. Coins you earn through gameplay or ads are immediately credited to your account and do not expire.
Rewarded ads are fully optional video advertisements. Tap the "Watch Ad" button in the menu or post-game screen, watch the full video (usually 15–30 seconds), and your coins will be added instantly once the ad finishes. You can watch multiple rewarded ads per day, subject to availability. If no rewarded ad is available, try again after a few minutes — ad inventory refreshes regularly.
First, make sure you have a stable internet connection and restart the app — purchases sometimes take a moment to sync. If coins still haven't appeared, use the Restore Purchases button in the game's settings menu. This will re-sync all previous purchases to your account. If the issue persists after restoring, please contact our support team with your order confirmation from Apple or Google and we'll resolve it promptly.
Ad availability depends on your region, device, and current ad network inventory. Common reasons ads may not show include: poor internet connection, reaching your daily ad limit (try again later), having an active ad blocker or VPN, or regional ad inventory being temporarily low. Ensure you have a stable internet connection and try disabling any VPN. If the problem continues, please contact support.
To restore previous purchases, open Perfect Tap, go to Settings, and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID (iOS) or Google Play account (Android) you used when you originally made the purchase. Purchases are linked to your store account — not the device — so they can be recovered on any device using the same account.
We take bugs seriously and appreciate your help! Please use the contact form below and select "Bug Report" as the issue category. Include as much detail as possible: your device model, OS version, a description of what happened, and steps to reproduce the issue if possible. Screenshots or screen recordings are very helpful. We review all bug reports and aim to issue fixes in the next app update.
Perfect Tap: Reflex Challenge supports iOS 14.0 or later (iPhone and iPad) and Android 6.0 (Marshmallow) or later. For the best experience, we recommend keeping your device updated to the latest OS version and ensuring you have the latest version of the app installed from the App Store or Google Play Store.

Self-Help Guide

Troubleshooting

Try these steps in order before reaching out — they resolve most common issues.

1

Restart the App

Fully close Perfect Tap and reopen it. This clears temporary glitches and refreshes your connection to our servers.

2

Update to the Latest Version

Open the App Store or Google Play Store and check if an update is available for Perfect Tap. Updates contain bug fixes and performance improvements.

3

Check Your Internet Connection

Some features (ads, purchases, leaderboards) require an active internet connection. Switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data to test which is working better.

4

Restart Your Device

A full device restart can resolve memory issues, stale background processes, and connectivity problems that a simple app restart cannot fix.

5

Reinstall the App

If the issue persists, uninstall and reinstall Perfect Tap from the App Store or Google Play. Note: reinstalling does not affect your purchases — use Restore Purchases after reinstalling.

6

Contact Support

Still stuck? Send us a message using the form below. We respond within 24–72 hours and will work with you until the issue is resolved.

How We Use Your Data

Perfect Tap uses Google AdMob to serve ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as Advertising ID / IDFA) to deliver relevant advertisements and measure ad performance. This data is handled in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases are processed through Apple App Store or Google Play Store — we never store your payment information. We use basic analytics to understand how the game is played and to improve your experience. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.
